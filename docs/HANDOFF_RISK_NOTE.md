# AlphaIQ™ Risk Parameter Handoff Note

Historical discussions included aggressive retail risk preferences. They are context, not safe production defaults. The successor must keep risk parameters explicit/configurable and should establish release-appropriate defaults through documented risk policy rather than copying an old percentage into code.

Any material live capital/risk policy that is not already authoritatively approved is an owner decision. Regardless of parameter values, risk remains an independent fail-safe veto and missing required policy must not default to permissive execution.
