# AlphaIQ™ Empirical Report Requirements

For each adequately sampled OOS engine×instrument slice, preserve the dimensions regime, strategy/version, canonical instrument, thesis timeframe, entry timeframe, session, event state and fold. Report sample size and uncertainty before interpreting performance.

At minimum include expectancy/confidence bounds, win rate, payoff ratio, Profit Factor, drawdown, explicit Sharpe/Sortino semantics, MFE/MAE, capture efficiency, slippage/latency assumptions and regime-mismatch degradation. Add KPM-12 annualised/robustness metrics only under their explicit policy assumptions.

Show excluded/abstained cells and reasons; do not hide insufficient samples. Separate train/validation development results from OOS results. Identify historical coverage and data/provider lineage. Report limitations such as missing spreads/ticks/events or synthetic execution assumptions. Do not translate research rankings into guarantees or unsupported broker superiority claims.
