# AlphaIQ™ Successor Success Criteria

A strong successor outcome is not “more code.” It is demonstrable closure of the evidence and operational gaps while preserving the existing architecture.

Success means: materially increased certified strategy coverage from exact sources; genuine quality-controlled multi-asset historical evidence; reproducible OOS/robustness/mismatch outputs; governed ML only where supported; risk/execution recovery proven; KPM-15/16/17 resolved to their stated acceptance criteria; paper/shadow behavior documented; independent audit findings closed; and a fresh Contabo host reproduces the approved release package. Unsupported items remain explicitly blocked rather than cosmetically marked complete.
