# AlphaIQ™ Instrument Universe Handoff

Gold is deliberately first in research priority, not the only asset. Preserve canonical XAUUSD, XAUGBP, XAUEUR, XAUJPY and configurable additional Gold crosses, XAGUSD, and the liquid FX universe already represented in `instrument_intelligence.py`.

Do not encode the final broker venue into canonical identity. Broker aliases/routes are configuration. Evidence should be capable of revealing which strategy/instrument/timeframe/session/regime combination is productive and how that engine deteriorates outside its preferred regime.

Do not select a broker or pair merely because an example appears in tests. Specialisation becomes a release configuration only when supported by applicable evidence and execution-quality constraints.
