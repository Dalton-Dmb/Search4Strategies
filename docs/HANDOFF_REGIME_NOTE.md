# AlphaIQ™ Regime Taxonomy Handoff

Current operational research states are:

- Strong Trend
- Weak Trend
- Range / Mean Reversion
- Compression
- Expansion
- Transition
- Event Shock
- Chop

UNKNOWN/abstention remains essential when evidence is insufficient or materially ambiguous. Thresholds/persistence/hysteresis are versioned policy/configuration concerns; do not insert arbitrary constants merely to force every observation into a regime. Range/mean-reversion is a first-class strategy/regime family, not a fallback after trend strategies fail.
