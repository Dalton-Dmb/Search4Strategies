# AlphaIQ™ Abstention Handoff Policy

Abstention is a valid system output, not an error to eliminate. AlphaIQ™ should abstain/stand aside when required market history is missing, features are stale/insufficient, regime evidence is unknown/ambiguous, strategy logic is uncertified/ineligible, statistical evidence is inadequate, risk vetoes, execution quality fails policy, or a required release/live gate is absent.

Journal the reason with enough lineage to analyse abstention frequency later. Do not convert UNKNOWN/FLAT/NO_TRADE into a directional trade merely to increase trade count or backtest coverage.
