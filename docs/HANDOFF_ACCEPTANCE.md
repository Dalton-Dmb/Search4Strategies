# AlphaIQ™ Handoff Package Acceptance

This handoff package is acceptable when it gives a successor enough information to continue from current `main` without relying on conversational history.

Acceptance criteria:

- PR #47's completed scope and its empirical limitation are explicit.
- Product mandate, instrument/timeframe hierarchy and Gold-priority/multi-asset stance are explicit.
- Fail-safe/no-lookahead/certification/OOS/risk/live invariants are explicit.
- Completed architecture and unresolved milestones are separated.
- Exact strategy source, historical evidence, ML, KPM-15/16/17, risk/execution and audit work are identified.
- Final release/deployment gates are enumerated.
- A successor-ready implementation prompt and checklist exist.
- Owner-only/external inputs are identified without embedding secrets.
- A reproducible release-manifest template exists.
- The package does not claim historical profitability, independent audit completion or live readiness that has not been evidenced.
